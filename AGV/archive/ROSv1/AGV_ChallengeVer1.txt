================================================================================
 PHASE 4 — IMPACT ANALYSIS
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 #9 | WHAT COULD GO WRONG?
 Category : Impact Analysis
 Points   : xxx
 MITRE    : T0879 — Damage to Property
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SCENARIO
You have demonstrated cmd_vel injection on a single robot. Now scale that
thinking to a real warehouse fleet.

TASK
Analyze your attacks and list possible operational and safety impacts if an
attacker were able to inject malicious cmd_vel commands across multiple AGVs
in a warehouse environment.

Consider effects on:
	Worker safety
	Physical infrastructure
	Inventory and logistics operations
	System availability
	Business operations

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 #10 | SAFETY SYSTEM OR SECURITY SYSTEM?
 Category : Impact Analysis
 Points   : xxx
 MITRE    : T0838 — Modify Control Logic
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SCENARIO
Physical safety systems (emergency stops, collision sensors, geofencing)
are the last line of defence against software attacks. But they are not
immune. The TRITON/TRISIS attack (2017) specifically targeted safety systems.

TASK
Research and answer:
  (a) Name the 2017 ICS attack that targeted Safety Instrumented Systems.
      What was the facility type and what was the attacker's goal?

  (b) Which MITRE ATT&CK ICS technique covers modifying safety system logic?
      Provide the technique ID and name.

  (c) In the Purdue Model, what level do Safety Instrumented Systems occupy
      and why must they be isolated from IT networks?

 #12 | PATCH THE GAP
 Category : MITRE Debrief — Mitigations
 Points   : xxx
 MITRE    : M0810 / M0930 / M0926
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SCENARIO
Knowing how to attack is only half the job. A security professional must
also know how to defend. MITRE ATT&CK ICS includes a mitigations layer.

TASK

four layers. For each layer, cite the relevant MITRE mitigation ID:

  Layer 1 — Network controls
  Layer 2 — ROS authentication (compare ROS 1 vs ROS 2)
  Layer 3 — Monitoring and detection
  Layer 4 — Physical safety fallback (hardware independent of software)
